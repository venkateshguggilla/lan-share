LAN Share - before you install

WHAT THIS INSTALLER CHANGES ON YOUR PC
- Installs LAN Share for all users (default: C:\Program Files\LAN Share) and adds
  Start menu and desktop shortcuts.
- Adds a Windows Firewall rule named "LAN Share" that lets other devices on your
  PRIVATE (home/office) networks connect to LAN Share. It does not apply to Public
  networks. Uninstalling LAN Share removes this rule.
- When you first open LAN Share, it sets itself to start when you sign in to
  Windows and keeps running in the taskbar tray, so other devices can find this
  PC. Turn either off in LAN Share > Settings > Running in the background.

LAN Share only accepts connections when you have set a password, and only shows
the folders you choose to share. By default it also asks you to accept or reject
each new device. It sends nothing to the internet: files go directly between
devices on your network, and only when you ask.

UNINSTALLING
Settings > Apps > Installed apps > LAN Share > Uninstall. This removes the app,
its shortcuts, the firewall rule and the start-at-sign-in entry. Your settings
stay in %APPDATA%\LAN Share; delete that folder to remove them too.
